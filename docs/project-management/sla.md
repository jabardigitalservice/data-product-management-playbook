# Service Level Agreement

## Tujuan Penentuan SLA

**Improved data quality**: By defining clear expectations, domain teams are incentivized to prioritize data quality 

**Enhanced data consumer trust**: Users can rely on data products with known quality and performance levels 

**Data-driven decision making**: Reliable data with defined SLAs enables better informed business decisions

## Key Points

### Domain ownership:

Each domain team is responsible for setting SLAs for their data products, aligning with the specific needs of their business area. 

### Data as a product
The SLA concept emphasizes that data is treated as a product with defined quality and delivery standards, not just a byproduct of operations. 

### Transparency and accountability:
Clear SLAs promote transparency about data quality and hold domain teams accountable for meeting performance expectations. 


## Metrics and KPIs

### Data Product Cycle Time

How long it takes for a data product to go from development to delivery (e.g., 2-4 weeks)

Typical SLA per lifecycle stage:

- Bootstrap: 4-6 weeks
- Pre-Development: 4-6 weeks
- Design: 4-6 weeks
- Development: 4-6 weeks
- Delivery: 2-4 weeks
- Monitoring and Evaluation: 4-6 weeks


### Data Freshness

How frequently data is updated and made available to consumers (e.g., near real-time, hourly updates)


### Data availability

The percentage of time a data product is accessible to users 

### Data accuracy

Acceptable levels of data errors or inconsistencies 

### Latency

Response time for data access queries 
Data quality metrics: Specific measures of data quality relevant to the domain (e.g., completeness, consistency)
